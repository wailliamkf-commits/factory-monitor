# Decisions

- D001: Independent repository, branch implement/v1; no edits to FDE or ChatGPT source mirror.
- D002: The accepted in-chat plan is the approved spec. Execute continuously; no additional design approval gates.
- D003: User cost routing overrides per-task multi-review and five-fix-loop skill defaults: two same-category failures trigger diagnosis; one concentrated final independent review.
- D004: Field acceptance will remain FAIL until actual Windows/Mac footage, switching and 72h evidence exist. Synthetic replay may validate software behavior only.
- D005: Dependency environment uses bundled Python3.12; no global Python changes. No paid cloud inference, no real camera upload. Local model download permitted for authorized implementation.
- D006: Independent review demonstrated that generic global clicks cannot prove the actual target under overlays and coordinate scaling. Runtime OS-click dispatch is disabled unconditionally. Automatic enlargement/return remains undelivered until a target-client adapter is implemented and validated; manual client operation is the explicit fallback, with unavailable coverage shown.
- D007: Freshness is per camera. A shared monitoring-client clock cannot establish ten fresh feeds; missing/stale camera-specific heartbeat proof disables that camera's analysis and records evidence gaps.
- D008: A real local Qwen ten-event synthetic burst missed the frozen SLA (1 completed, 9 timeouts). Preserve all failures. Do not change the 15-second deadline, remove cameras, or suppress review to claim success. A bounded component concurrency diagnostic does not replace this runtime result or justify a hardware purchase. The minimum necessary hardware tier and current quote remain undetermined until the Windows host and actual capture/model workload are measured.
- D009: Delivery label is engineering preview v0.1, not accepted V1. Missing automatic switching, review capacity and target-OS/field evidence are mandatory work, not OPTIONAL/BACKLOG. Further unrelated polish, twenty cameras, mobile push and commercialization remain out of scope.
